# Cold e-mails to researchers / engineers (templates)

Used by the "cold emails" command (claude/jobs.md). Under 150 words, one specific paper or project of theirs, one clear ask, ends with "CV attached". Facts about you only from profile/master_profile.md; never invent an address.

## Template
Subject: [Their paper/project]: question from a [your role] working on [your field]

Dear Dr. [Last name],

I read your [venue year] paper "[title]" and [one specific, genuine sentence about what you found useful or interesting].

I'm a [your role] at [your school/company] working on [your field, one line], most recently [your most relevant result, one line, from your profile].

I'm applying for [role type] at [Company], and I'd be grateful for [a 15-minute chat / advice on the team / a pointer to the right person]. [Optional: the job link.]

Thank you for your time,
[Your name]
CV attached

## Follow-up (once, ~10 days later, as a reply to the same thread)
Dear Dr. [Last name], a short follow-up in case my note got buried. I'd still value [the ask]. Thank you either way.
