# Referral request templates (LinkedIn or text)

*Your agent drafts; you send. Always include the **job ID and link**, so the referrer can submit it in two minutes. Facts only from profile/master_profile.md.*

## A. Friend at the company
Hey [Name]! Hope things are going well at [Company]. I'm applying for [role type / season], and there's an opening on your side that fits my work:
**[Job title]**, Req [ID]: [link]
Would you be comfortable referring me? I've attached my résumé, and here's a 2-line summary you can paste into the referral form:
> [your one-line pitch, tailored to the role]

No pressure at all if it's awkward. Thanks!

## B. Someone you met once or twice (a conference, a talk, a class)
Hi [Name], it was great talking with you at [event] about [topic]. I'm now looking for [role type], and I noticed [Company] has an opening that lines up closely with my work on [your field]:
**[Job title]**, Req [ID]: [link]
If you think I'd be a reasonable fit, would you consider referring me or pointing me to the hiring manager? I've attached my résumé.
Thanks, [Your first name]

## C. Follow-up (once, about 7 days later, only if no reply)
Hi [Name], just floating this back up in case it got buried. Totally understand if you're busy. Thanks either way!
